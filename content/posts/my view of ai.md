my thoughts of ai being similar to slaver still holds. but my i want to tackle
the argument that ai is just another tool for creativity. it is unfortunate that
the initial "use-case" for ai is to replace human creativity. it is able to
present an illusion of being creative since it is inherently unable to "think"
as humans do. they are imprecise and they don't have an understanding of the
meaning behind the words we use and thus they produce unexpected results and we
hail them as "creative".

ai is not a tool. a tool allows the user to have more control over the resulting
product. it allows the person to have more levers to be able to express their
creativity, their intent. ai is often compared to the camera replacing painters
but this is comparison is flawed. painting is one way to capture reality and the
controls available to the painter is their brush, paint, canvas, and their own
skills. cameras allow people to have other levers to express their intent of
capturing reality. instead of paints, cameras use negatives or whatever cameras
use (need more research). does it make it easier to capture reality? yes. but it
allows for the artist to focus on different aspects of the image like
composition, color grading, exposures and types of lenses.  

now how does ai differ? it can be argued that the new controls to express intent
is your prompts. ignoring the unethical way that ai are trained and just
consider it's utility. since it doesn't have the same understanding with words
that humans do, the words in the prompt does not correlate directly to the
output. you can say that make the box "pop" and the output will be a totally new
image with the bomb emoji. LLMs allow users to use the a human language to
create an output. the problem with that is human languages evolve and words mean
different things depending on the culture or generation. it is a poor way to
create an output that accurately represents the experiences of the user. the
prompt will be processed with the semantics of the training data. there is a
reality that an asian person wanting an image that depicts an normal asian
person doing normal asian things. then the output image depicts the asian person
stereotypical things that a racist ignorant person would expect. this could
happen if the ai is trained on data that doesn't correct for biases.

(i should probably not talk about what it might do, but what ai allows people to
do)
if all ai is corrected to be perfectly unbiased, then it will just be the most average of everything, bland. it would probably say that the best color is beige.

ai allows people to use the english language to produce some output. however, i don't think that languages are design for that. it is designed to communicate meaning between human brains. my argument is that ai cannot be called a tool if the controls are a step removed from the output. an ai that detects cancer from images is a tool since the input are images which is directly correlated to the diagnosis of the ai and is *deterministic*. this should not be confused to with human art not being deterministic, it is not. the means(controls) by which the art is created is deterministic.
